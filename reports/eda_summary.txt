== How to read these results ==
* Correlation shows association only. It does NOT show that one variable causes another.
* Country-years from the same country are not independent, so pooled correlations are rough guides.
* No country is declared 'best for startups'. National averages say little about
  a single startup: funding, talent, regulation, market access and sector all matter.
